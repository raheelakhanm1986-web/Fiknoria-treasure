Our story

Born in the classroom, made for every home

Fiknoria Treasure began with a simple belief: every child is born curious, and the right materials help that curiosity grow. After years of working with young children as a Montessori directress, I saw how much they love simple, beautiful, hands-on learning.

That is why I started creating original educational ebooks, colouring books and printables, so parents and teachers everywhere can bring joyful, Montessori-inspired learning to their children, one little discovery at a time.
